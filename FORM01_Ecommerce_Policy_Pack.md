## 2. Shipping & Delivery Policy

### 2.1 Service area

We currently intend to ship to serviceable pincodes within India.
Availability is confirmed at checkout. We may be unable to deliver to
some locations due to courier coverage or operational restrictions.

### 2.2 Order processing

Orders are normally prepared for dispatch within **2--4 business days**
after successful payment and order confirmation. Business days exclude
Sundays and public holidays unless otherwise stated. This is an
estimate, not a guaranteed dispatch deadline.

Limited drops, pre-orders, high order volumes, quality checks or other
operational circumstances may affect processing time. If a material
delay occurs, we will make reasonable efforts to communicate it.

### 2.3 Estimated delivery

After dispatch, delivery is estimated to take **3--7 business days**,
depending on the destination, courier service and local conditions.
Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Remote locations, public
holidays, weather, transport disruption, address issues or other events
outside our reasonable control may cause delays.

### 2.4 Shipping charges and tracking

Any shipping charge will be shown at checkout before payment. Where
tracking is available, we will share the tracking information through
the contact channel provided with your order.

### 2.5 Correct delivery details

Please verify your name, phone number, email and complete delivery
address before placing an order. If an address or contact detail is
incorrect, contact **support@form01.com** as soon as possible. We cannot
guarantee changes after an order has been processed or handed to the
courier.

### 2.6 Delivery attempts and undelivered parcels

Please ensure someone can receive the parcel or follow the courier's
delivery instructions. If a parcel is returned to us because the address
is incomplete, delivery is repeatedly unsuccessful, or the parcel is not
collected, contact support to discuss available options. Any reshipping
charge, if applicable, will be communicated before it is applied and
will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

### 2.7 Lost, damaged or incorrect deliveries

If your parcel appears lost, arrives damaged, or contains an incorrect
item, contact **support@form01.com** promptly and include your order
number and relevant photographs where possible. We will investigate with

### 3.1 Seven-day return window

to the conditions below. Contact **support@form01.com** with your order
number and reason for the request. The return window is measured from
the date the order is delivered.

This voluntary return policy does not limit any rights or remedies
available under applicable consumer law, including where a product is
defective, damaged, incorrect, materially different from its
description, or otherwise legally non-conforming.

### 3.2 Return condition

For a standard change-of-mind return, the item must be:

-   Unworn, unwashed and unused, except for reasonable inspection or
    try-on.
-   In its original condition, with original tags, labels and included
    accessories.
-   Returned with the order reference and any return instructions we
    provide.

We may ask for photographs or other information to assess a reported
issue. We will not use this process to deny a remedy that is required by
law.

### 3.3 Size exchanges

We offer a **size exchange request** within the same 7-calendar-day
window, subject to stock availability and the item meeting the
applicable condition requirements. Contact support before sending an
item back so we can confirm the next steps.

If the requested size is unavailable, we will explain the available
options, which may include another available size or a refund where
applicable. We will not promise an exchange until availability is
confirmed.

materially different from its description, contact

confirmed. A payment authorisation, pending status or screenshot alone

reversal or refund. Bank and payment-provider processing times may vary.

### 5.4 Order cancellation requests

possible with your order number. We will try to stop processing or

guaranteed once an order has been packed, handed to the courier or
otherwise progressed beyond the cancellation point.

If we cannot cancel the order, you may still request a return in
accordance with our Returns, Exchanges & Refunds Policy and applicable
law. Nothing in this section removes a legal right to cancellation or
refund.

### 5.5 Refunds
